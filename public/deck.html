The problem

Field intelligence is trapped across calls, emails, and Slack — and reps won't do the CRM admin to surface it. So the CRM goes stale, no one has a true account 360, and the deals quietly rotting look fine on paper.

By the time that insight reaches marketing, product, and leadership through call notes and QBRs, the deal has already moved. Reps shouldn't have to do extra work to share it.

Where we start · and why it comes first

De-risk the one hard thing: trustworthy extraction.

Everything downstream — scoring, divergence, routing — is only as good as the step that turns a messy conversation into a specific field on a specific account. So that's the first and only thing we make genuinely hard-tested.

The guardrail
Every extracted signal must quote the transcript verbatim, or it's dropped — checked in code, not trusted to the model.
Measured
We planted a hidden answer key in the data, so we can grade extraction field-by-field: 90% accuracy.
Everything else
Deterministic, testable code — auditable and cheap. Not a black box.

How the intelligence lands · and stays trustworthy

It doesn't just score. It lands on the account — reviewably.

The receipt
Each field shows the source conversation → the field it populated → the verbatim quote behind it. That's how scraped data gets into the right spot and stays usable. Absent fields show greyed — absence is signal.
Human-gated
Nothing auto-writes to the CRM. Every suggestion is Accept / Reject — inline or from a bulk ops queue. Approve → "would sync to Salesforce ✓." Every decision is labeled training data.

Answers the first question ops asks — "aren't you worried the AI corrupts the CRM?" It can't; every write is reversible and human-approved.

Act III · Zero rep effort

Reps do nothing extra. The system reads what already happens.

  • Leadership defines what "qualified" means; every deal is graded against that rubric from the field, with evidence — no forms, no self-reporting.
  • Runs on a ~5-minute batch, so intelligence reaches the right team while it's still actionable.
  • 151 signals extracted this window; 11 high-value actions routed; the rest logged. Clustering + an alert-fatigue governor keep it from becoming noise.

Why you can trust it · and what's real in this demo

Built for the failure modes, not the happy path.

Failure modes designed for
Hallucination → verbatim-quote guardrail. Alert fatigue → clustering + recipient caps. False divergence → confidence + evidence required. Staleness → recency decay. Black box → reason codes everywhere.
The honest demo split
Data is synthetic. The extraction agent is production-shaped and runs live on demand (the Live-extract tab). For this walkthrough the committed signals come from a deterministic stand-in with the identical guardrail — swap in a key and it's live, nothing downstream changes.
