# GTM Signal Engine

The GTM Signal Engine turns evidence-backed field signals into coordinated, read-only recommendations for product, marketing, and sales. A single conversation can reveal a feature gap, a competitor pattern, and a winning play; the context graph connects those signals to the teams and campaigns that can act.

## Product surfaces

- **Digest** (/digest): VP Sales Engineering and VP ABM “what’s working / not working” findings.
- **Fan-out** (/action): source quote → detected pattern → product, marketing, and sales recommendations.
- **Macro** (/heatmap): product × competitor, campaign × segment, and product × campaign rate views with n suppression and density warnings.
- **Teams** (/teams): feature and campaign break-outs by team and segment.
- **Account** (/account/:id): read-only two-dial health/progression and evidence drill-down.

The product never writes to CRM, triggers Marketo, or asks a user to approve an action. Mode chips describe whether a recommendation would auto-fire or is routed for the owning team. Trust comes from evidence provenance: every extracted signal is anchored to a verbatim source quote.

## Honest architecture

- Data is synthetic and generated by scripts/generate.py.
- Extraction is the one LLM step. Without ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, deterministic mock extraction produces the same evidence-quote contract.
- Analytics, scoring, heatmaps, rollups, and fan-out are deterministic and unit-tested.
- The analytics book contains 600 opportunities; only the smaller conversation sample feeds extraction.
